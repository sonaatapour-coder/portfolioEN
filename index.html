03 · Why It Matters

Your website is part of your business.

A website isn't just a collection of pages — it's often the first real interaction someone has with your business. A good one should:

  • Build credibility from the first second
  • Communicate value clearly, without friction
  • Make information easy to find and understand
  • Work well on every device, not just desktop
  • Load quickly, everywhere
  • Guide visitors toward taking action

04 · About

A developer who cares about the details.

I'm a freelance web designer and developer working with businesses that want a website which actually represents them — not a template with their logo on it. My focus sits at the intersection of design and engineering: interfaces that look considered and code that holds up.

I work across modern web design, UI/UX, and frontend development, with an emphasis on responsive layouts, performance, and clean, SEO-friendly structure. I write code I'd be comfortable handing to another developer, and I design screens I'd be comfortable putting in front of a client.
